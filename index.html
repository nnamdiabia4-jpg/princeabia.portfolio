About

All my life, I’ve always loved design, from drawing portraits of my parents to designing Afro-art posters to creating brand design systems and art direction, and now exploring the world of product engineering. I have been in the design world for the past 4 years. My work sits around logo & brand design systems, art direction plus a touch of product design. When collaborating or solo designing, I bring a clear design process, clear attention to detail and a problem-solving approach to every project. When I’m not designing, I love yapping about everything crypto, tech, community and football.
